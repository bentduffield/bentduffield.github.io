§1

Research

My work sits at the intersection of corpus linguistics and legal interpretation. I use corpus methods to examine ordinary-meaning doctrine and statutory textualism in addition to examining the viability of corpus methods for legal interpretation.

My thesis studies intercoder reliability in corpus linguistics for legal interpretation by comparing coders of different backgrounds to determine best practices for replicable LCL studies. Results will inform the viability of LCL for use by judges and their clerks as opposed to sole reliance on a narrow range of experts with advanced training in both law and linguistics.
